6. SOCIAL AND VIDEO STRATEGY

Suggested publishing cadence:

* Four Reels/Shorts per week
* Two DJ/music clips per week, which can overlap with the above
* Stories three to five days per week
* Two longer YouTube videos per month
* Two website editorial updates per month

Use original footage wherever possible.

A longer video should be repurposed into several short clips, stories and website content.

2. Context
3. Demonstration or story
4. Personal editorial perspective
5. Relevant call to action
